U.S. Automotive Retail: New and Used Car Dealers

Industry study · RESECON 490AI, AI in Economics, UMass Amherst · Fall 2026 · In progress, final report due December 2026

A study of U.S. new and used car dealers (NAICS 441110 and 441120): how large the industry is, whether it is growing or contracting, what stage it is in, and where generative AI is showing up in dealerships. Data so far comes from the Bureau of Labor Statistics Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages, 2015 to 2025, with trade, company and academic sources being checked.
